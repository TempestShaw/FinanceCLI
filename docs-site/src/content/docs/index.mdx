## Your question. Your agent. The evidence behind its answer.

After [setting up the CLI and skill](/FinanceCLI/ai/), give your agent a question like this:

```text
Research Apple's latest annual filing with Finance CLI. Explain how it
makes money and three risks it discloses. Cite the filing date and source
for each finding, and tell me where the evidence is incomplete.
```

Your agent chooses the tools, follows up on the evidence, and writes the explanation. Finance CLI supplies filing excerpts, structured data, and calculation results. You can inspect the sources and rerun the commands behind the answer.

Requires an agent that can run local commands. The skill teaches it how to use the CLI; the CLI does not include an AI model or a hosted chat service.

## Why Finance CLI belongs in your research toolkit

Company research involves finding the right filing, locating a table, checking the reporting period, and calculating a metric. Repeating that work for each question means more downloads, copied numbers, and one-off scripts. Finance CLI makes those steps reusable for both you and your agent.

## Know what you are reading

Filing commands retain source identifiers such as URLs and accessions when available. Calculations expose their inputs and method. Provider failures and missing data remain visible. Read the [trust guide](/FinanceCLI/trust/).

Finance CLI retrieves evidence and performs calculations. Interpreting a filing or writing a summary is your task, or your agent's task. Combine the commands freely for your research question.
